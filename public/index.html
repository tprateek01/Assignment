Common questions

What kind of work can I request?

Any writing work: assignments, notes, lab records, reports, letters and more, handwritten or typed. You can also request diagrams and drawings such as engineering drawing.

Do you do diagrams and engineering drawings?

Yes. Choose "Diagrams & drawings" in the request form. This work is priced higher than regular writing because it takes much more time and care, so the price is a custom quote based on your instructions and the number of sheets. Please send the request at least 7 days before your deadline.

What about copies, registers and other material?

Plain A4 blank sheets are included. For anything else (a copy, register, file, drawing sheets, special paper or any other material), you pay the actual market price for whatever is used, with no extra margin. If you prefer, hand the material over beforehand at the pickup location instead, and no material cost applies.

How is the price decided?

I read your instructions and send a final price (per page for writing, a custom quote for diagrams and drawings). You can accept it or reply in the chat to negotiate. A close deadline can add a rush charge, which is shown as its own line in my offer so you can see exactly what it is.

Can I upload a PDF to get it written?

Yes. Attach your PDF in the request form (up to 8 MB), or add it later from the chat. I will write it out from that file.

Can I see how much is done?

Yes. While I work, the number of finished pages is updated live in your chat.

Where do I collect my finished work?

From Knowledge Park 2 Metro Station, Gate No. 2, Greater Noida. Open in Google Maps. We confirm the pickup time with you on WhatsApp. You can also hand over your own copy or register at the same place beforehand.

When does WhatsApp come in?

After you accept the final price. A WhatsApp button then appears here so we can finish the order and payment.

How do I come back to my chat?

Open this website and log in with the same email. We send you a 6-digit code, and all your requests are listed at the top of this section, on any phone or browser.
